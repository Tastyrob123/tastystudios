Since 2014 we have been collecting techniques, ingredients and ideas all over the world. We cook with them in Hamburg and on the North Sea, using what grows, swims and grazes here.

01Wanderlust

Eight countries,one kitchen.

Mexico, Peru, Japan, Korea, Portugal, Lebanon, Morocco, Italy. Something from every journey stays in the kitchen.

Laura's Portuguese roots bring lightness and Mediterranean warmth to the dishes and meet Jo's wanderlust flavours, which came about around the world in ten years of Salt & Silver. The result is a cuisine that is locally rooted and globally inspired.

05Origin

We knowwhere it comes from.

Regional ingredients, seasonal produce, fresh fish, game from Jagdgut Damp. The char comes from Birgit and Gunnar Reese's fish farm in Sarlhusen and goes onto the grill butterfly-cut. Beef and lamb come from pastures in North Frisia, chillies and herbs from our own fields.
