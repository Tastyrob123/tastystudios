Description

Salt & Silver am Meer: on the beach of St. Peter-Ording since 2022. After many requests and wishes, our crew clothes from the restaurant by the sea are now available for you too. The crewnecks carry our "Salt & Silver am Meer" illustration on the back, which we designed ourselves.

Details
  • Colour: dark blue
  • Round neckline
  • Ribbing at neck, sleeves and hem
  • Double-stitched seams
  • Soft, brushed inside
  • 70 % cotton, 30 % polyester, 290 g/m²
  • Wash with similar colours, wash and iron inside out, do not iron the print

We deliver within Germany, to the EU, to Switzerland and to the United Kingdom. Shipping is a flat rate per order.
