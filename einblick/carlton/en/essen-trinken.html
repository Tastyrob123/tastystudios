Seasonal &regional cuisine.

In our hotel restaurant, the Hansen-Ritschard-Saal, you enjoy our generous breakfast in the morning. During the day and in the evening we serve a range of dishes and menus.

Buying fresh produce is a matter of trust. The Blaue Kuh butcher’s supplies us with well-aged, tender meat, Gourmador delivers fresh vegetables and crisp fruit every day, and Bäckerei Michel provides fresh bread.

Oursuppliers.

We serve only local and seasonal produce, to avoid long transport routes. Our main suppliers from the region:

  1. 01

    Rugenbräu

    Matten near Interlaken

    The beer on our menu: Rugenbräu as Stange, Kübel and Hergöttli, plus Spezial light, dark and alcohol-free.

  2. 02

    Die Blaue Kuh butcher

    Matten near Interlaken

    The Blaue Kuh butcher shop provides us with well-aged and tender meats.

  3. 03

    Weinhandlung Ritschard AG

    Interlaken

    The roots of the wine merchant in Interlaken go back to 1840.

  4. 04

    H&R Gastro

    Interlaken

    H&R supplies us with coffee and dry goods.

  5. 05

    Gourmador

    Unterseen · Fruit & vegetables

    Gourmador delivers fresh vegetables and crisp fruit to us every day.

  6. 06

    Käsehaus K3

    Burgdorf · Musikkäse

    Our cheese supplier is Beat Wampfler from Burgdorf.

  7. 07

    Bäckerei Michel

    Unterseen

    Fresh bread comes from the Michel bakery.
