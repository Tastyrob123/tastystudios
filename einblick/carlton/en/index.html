TheVintage Hotel.

At the Carlton-Europe Vintage hotel for grown-ups, open all year and awarded four stars by HotellerieSuisse, we give you a warm welcome in every season.

Wonderfully located near Interlaken Ost station, with views of the world-famous Jungfrau massif, the surrounding Bernese Alps and the gently flowing Aare. The former Hotel Carlton and the Villa Europe have been one house since 2006.
