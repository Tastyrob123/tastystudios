In our hotel restaurant, delicious regional and seasonal cuisine and a wide selection of Swiss wines await you.

Oursuppliers.

We serve only local and seasonal produce, to avoid long transport routes. Our main suppliers from the region:

  1. 01

    Rugenbräu

    Matten near Interlaken

    The beer on our menu: Rugenbräu as Stange, Kübel and Hergöttli, plus Spezial light, dark and alcohol-free.

  2. 02

    Die Blaue Kuh butcher

    Matten near Interlaken

    The Blaue Kuh butcher shop provides us with well-aged and tender meats.

  3. 03

    Weinhandlung Ritschard AG

    Interlaken

    The roots of the wine merchant in Interlaken go back to 1840.

  4. 04

    H&R Gastro

    Interlaken

    H&R supplies us with coffee and dry goods.

  5. 05

    Gourmador

    Unterseen · Fruit & vegetables

    Gourmador delivers fresh vegetables and crisp fruit to us every day.

  6. 06

    Käsehaus K3

    Burgdorf · Musikkäse

    Bäckerei Michel

    Unterseen

    Fresh bread comes from the Michel bakery.
