Seminarswith pleasure.

Planning a one-day event? Then calculate with the day package. Day packages can be booked for eight people or more.

Simple

Half a day in the seminar room with one coffee break and a business lunch.

Included are a projector with screen, pinboard, flipchart, facilitator’s kit, writing set and Wi-Fi throughout the hotel, plus table water and a fruit bowl in the plenary room.

CHF 75per person from

Productive

A full day in the seminar room with two coffee breaks and a business lunch.

The coffee breaks change daily with the season: coffee or tea, table water or fruit juice and a small snack. Technology and Wi-Fi as with Simple.

CHF 99per person from

Relax

A seminar day, a four-course dinner and one night in a Classic double room with breakfast buffet.

Superior rooms or Signature Suites on request. From 1 April to 30 September a high-season supplement of CHF 75 applies.

CHF 209per guest from
