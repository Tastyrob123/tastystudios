In summer

On the upper 6th floor are the gallery and the large roof terrace with plenty of deckchairs, tables and a fire bowl.

On the outdoor bed on the terrace you dream beneath the stars. Your stay ends with breakfast as soon as the sun’s rays kiss you awake.

In winter

Experience an unforgettable winter night in the cosy bed in the winter-proof but unheated pavilion with its glass roof.

A snug, warm night is guaranteed with our vintage hot-water bottles, cosy cherry-stone pillows and a large choice of warming fur blankets. In very cold weather you can choose one of the two bedrooms of the suite, which lies directly beneath the terrace.
