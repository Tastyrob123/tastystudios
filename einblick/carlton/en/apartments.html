Rooms & Suites

ServicedApartments.

Here at the Carlton-Europe Vintage hotel for grown-ups you will find a holiday apartment to feel at home in. The apartments can also be rented for longer stays.

Feelat home.

Our apartments have a separate bedroom, a bathroom with shower and/or bathtub, a hairdryer and an additional WC. The living area has a flat-screen TV with radio, free Wi-Fi and a modern kitchen with oven, hob, dishwasher and Nespresso coffee machine.

Each apartment also has a sunny outdoor seating area with a wonderful view of the mountains.
