Since 1898

Traditionsince 1898.

Hospitality has been lived in these two splendid buildings since 1898. For years now Stephan JJ. Maeder has carried on the tradition of these historic houses with heart and soul.

Over 125 yearsof vintage.

125 years of evolution, a small excerpt in nine stages.

  1. 1898

    The Europe

    Construction of the northern part; the southern part followed eleven years later. Both buildings were erected under the ownership of Karl Alfred Ritschard, a former civil servant in Muri. The house was run by Alice Ritschard and Gertrud Biert.

  2. 1900

    The Carlton

    The Carlton house was built by Christian Hansen as the “Hotel Brünig et l’Univers”. It was open from April to October each year and, with private bathrooms, heating and a lift, was considered very modern at the time. Charles Affentranger bought it in 1947.

  3. 1952

    The first private bathroom

    In the Europe the first two rooms were fitted with a bath and WC. The shared bathrooms on each floor remained for some years, until the major conversion of 1966 gave every room its own bathroom and toilet.

  4. 1995

    Near the end?

    Francesca Comte acquired the Europe and thoroughly renovated the northern part. Sadly, the business did not do well enough. The Europe AG of the time was declared bankrupt and the hotel passed to UBS. There was even talk of demolishing the Art Nouveau building with its crumbling façade.

  5. 2001

    Bernensis Hotel AG

    Together with friends and colleagues, Stephan JJ. Maeder managed to found Bernensis Hotel AG and buy the hotel from UBS. All were prepared to forgo dividends and put in plenty of unpaid work to get the house back on an even keel.

  6. 2006

    Two houses, one hotel

    Bernensis Hotel AG took over the “Hotel Carlton” from Werner Affentranger. The two hotels were joined by a new reception building, and all the façades were restored in line with heritage requirements.

  7. 2016

    The annexe

    On the southern plot a new building went up with a spacious underground garage, a large dining room with a kitchen on the same level, apartments and a hotel garden with a natural swimming pond. Work paused each summer season so that guests could go on enjoying their peace and quiet.

  8. 2019

    Guests aged 16 and over

    Since January 2019 the Carlton-Europe has welcomed only hotel guests aged 16 and over, as one of the first adults-only hotels in Switzerland. In 2020 Stephan JJ. Maeder received the Milestone award for his innovative ideas during the crisis.

  9. 2022

    Four stars

    The hotel was classified as a 4-star hotel for the first time, and the annexe as 4-star serviced apartments. A photovoltaic system has been on the roof since 2024. In 2026 Bernensis Hotel AG celebrates its 25th anniversary.

From thearchive.

Two façade plans of the Hotel Carlton, inscribed “Herrn J. Roth, Interlaken”. The arcades on the ground floor and the tower at the corner still stand today.

Two houses,both protected.

Today’s Carlton was built in 1900 as the Hotel Brünig et l’Univers, to plans by the Interlaken architect Robert Forster, right next to the Europe, two years its senior. Since 2000 it has been on the heritage inventory of the Canton of Bern, and since March 2024 it has also been under federal protection.

During the Second World War the Europe served as a military medical facility and remained closed to guests. Around the turn of the millennium its demolition was on the table, until Stephan JJ. Maeder and his partners took over the Europe in 2001 and the Carlton in 2005.

“Our great concern was to save the two historic hotels.”
Stephan JJ. Maeder in “Hotelier” magazine

Our effortsbehind the scenes.

We currently hold the Swisstainable certification at level 1, “Committed”, so that guests are aware of our focus on sustainability from the moment they book.

Regional & seasonal

We serve only local and seasonal produce, to avoid long transport routes. Our drinks list features only Swiss wines.

Food Save

We encourage our guests to go to the buffet several times, and we serve smaller portions with free seconds.

Water & heat

We heat with district heating. Around the house, containers collect rainwater for the flowers in the garden. The natural swimming pond needs no chemicals.

Power from the roof

Since 2024 the photovoltaic system has produced enough energy for around 30 households. Two MOVE charging stations charge your electric car with up to 22 kW.

Arriving by train

With the SBB luggage special, suitcases are collected at home and are waiting in the hotel room on arrival. Instead of disposable slippers there are “Reuse-me” slippers.
